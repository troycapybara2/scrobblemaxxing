How picks are chosen

Each album is a consensus favorite on RYM-style charts for one of your lanes, a “fans of X also love” connection to artists you actually play, or a near-universal classic. Every pick is checked against your full scrobble history, so the sticker tells you whether you’ve never touched it, barely touched it, know the artist but not this album, or only half-heard it. “Best bets” weighs how much you play the artists it connects to.
